Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. Introduction

At Watchly, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our movie and TV show tracking service.

Key Point: Watchly is designed with privacy in mind. Most of your data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers unless you explicitly choose to use backup features.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide

When using Watchly, you may provide:

  • Movie and TV show titles you add to your watchlist
  • Viewing status and progress information
  • Personal ratings and notes
  • Watchlist rankings and preferences
  • Backup authentication tokens (e.g., GitHub tokens for Gist backup)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We do not collect personal information automatically. However, when you use certain features:

  • TMDB API Requests: When searching for movies/shows, requests are made to TMDB's servers (subject to their privacy policy)
  • Local Storage: Your browser stores your watchlist data locally on your device

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal identifying information (name, email, address, phone)
  • Device identifiers or tracking data
  • Location information
  • Browsing history outside of Watchly
  • Analytics or usage statistics

3. How We Store Your Data

3.1 Local Storage (Default)

By default, all your Watchly data is stored locally in your browser using:

  • Local Storage: Your watchlist, viewing history, and preferences
  • Session Storage: Temporary data during your browsing session
  • No Server Storage: We do not store your watchlist data on our servers

3.2 Optional Cloud Backup

If you choose to use backup features:

  • GitHub Gist: You may backup your data to your own GitHub Gist
  • Your Control: You control this backup through your own GitHub account
  • Third-Party Terms: GitHub's privacy policy and terms apply to this data

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the Watchly service:

  • Display your personal watchlist and viewing history
  • Track your viewing progress and preferences
  • Generate statistics about your viewing habits
  • Enable search and discovery of new content via TMDB
  • Facilitate backup and restore of your data (when you choose to use these features)

We do not:

  • Sell or share your data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising or marketing
  • Analyze your data for commercial purposes
  • Create user profiles for external use

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 The Movie Database (TMDB)

When you search for movies and TV shows, we use TMDB's API:

  • Search queries are sent to TMDB servers
  • TMDB provides movie/show metadata and images
  • TMDB's privacy policy applies to these interactions
  • We do not control TMDB's data practices

5.2 GitHub (Optional Backup)

If you use the GitHub Gist backup feature:

  • Your watchlist data is stored in your GitHub account
  • GitHub's privacy policy and terms of service apply
  • You can delete this backup data at any time through GitHub
  • We do not have access to your GitHub account beyond the specific Gist you choose to use

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Local Storage Security: Data is stored securely in your browser's protected storage
  • HTTPS: All connections to Watchly use encrypted HTTPS
  • No Central Database: We don't maintain a central database of user information
  • Token Security: Backup tokens are stored locally and encrypted by your browser

Your Responsibility: Since data is stored locally, you are responsible for:

  • Keeping your device secure
  • Backing up your data if desired
  • Managing your own backup service accounts (e.g., GitHub)

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the Watchly interface
  • Modify: You can edit or delete any watchlist items at any time
  • Export: You can export your complete watchlist data
  • Delete: You can clear all data using the "Reset All" feature
  • Portability: Your data is in standard JSON format and fully portable

8. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Remains on your device until you delete it or clear browser data
  • Backup Data: Stored according to the terms of your chosen backup service (e.g., GitHub Gist)
  • No Server Logs: We do not maintain server logs of your watchlist activity

9. Children's Privacy

Watchly is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use Watchly or provide any information through the service.

10. International Users

Watchly can be used globally. Since data is stored locally on your device by default, data protection laws in your jurisdiction apply to how your device handles this information. When using third-party services like TMDB or GitHub, their respective privacy policies and jurisdictional terms apply.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes:

  • We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • Significant changes will be communicated through the application interface
  • Continued use of Watchly after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the feedback options available in the application.

13. Summary

🔒 Your Privacy in Simple Terms:

  • Local First: Your watchlist stays on your device by default
  • No Tracking: We don't track you or collect personal information
  • Optional Backup: You control if/how you backup your data
  • Full Control: You can export, modify, or delete your data anytime
  • No Ads: We don't use your data for advertising or marketing
  • Transparent: All data practices are clearly explained in this policy
